Mettle App

The Mettle App accompanies Mettle, the offering of Suno Alta GmbH for mental strength. It will include a short journal, everyday exercises, guided yoga practices and meditations.

It is aimed at clients and students of Christof Rauber and will launch first in Switzerland, in German and English, for Android and iOS.
